## App Description

Build your fishing harbor, upgrade your boat, and catch rare fish across beautiful ocean areas in this relaxing fishing simulation.

### Full Description

Fishing Harbor is an offline fishing simulation with harbor building, boat upgrades, a fish encyclopedia, world map exploration, and 52 original Harbor Boost cards (Fishing, Recovery, Discovery, Utility) — custom artwork, not template graphics. Progress saves locally with no account required.
